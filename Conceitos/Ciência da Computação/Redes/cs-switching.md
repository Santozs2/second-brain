# 🔄 Network Switching

---

## 📊 Tipos

**Circuit Switching:** Reserva caminho (telefone)  
**Packet Switching:** Cada pacote independente (Internet)  
**Message Switching:** Armazena e encaminha  

---

## 🎯 Switch vs Router

**Switch:** Layer 2 (MAC), Local network  
**Router:** Layer 3 (IP), Entre redes
